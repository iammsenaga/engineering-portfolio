## The product

iBible brings Bible reading, passage search and personal study notes into one web application. It uses a Python backend to retrieve Bible content and manage accounts and saved notes.

## What I built

- A daily-verse landing page.
- Bible book, chapter and translation selection.
- Passage and keyword search.
- User registration and sign-in.
- Personal notes with a history view and deletion controls.

## Engineering considerations

The application combines external Bible-content services with local account and note storage. Access controls must protect personal notes, credentials must be handled safely, and external-service failures must be understandable to users.
