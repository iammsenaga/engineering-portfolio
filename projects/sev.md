## The problem

Running an election involves more than recording votes. Organizers need candidate screening, voter accreditation, clear schedules, administrative workflows and results that participants can follow.

## My contribution

I designed the product and built the frontend and backend across the election lifecycle: candidate applications, accreditation, voting, administration and public aggregate results.

## Product capabilities

- Candidate screening and election-management workflows.
- Voter accreditation and eligibility checks.
- Anonymous voting and aggregate results.
- Responsive interfaces for participants and election organizers.

## Engineering considerations

The design balances eligibility verification, ballot privacy, administrative accountability and usability on mobile devices. The backend is authoritative for election rules and state; the interface helps participants understand what they can do at each stage.

The public website is an operational product, not a test environment. This case study does not publish application source, verification internals or election data, and does not claim independent security certification.
