## The problem

Veterinary teams need patient history, appointments, clinical documentation and reporting to work together. Fragmented records make it harder to follow a case over time or learn from activity across the clinic.

## My contribution

I brought veterinary domain knowledge into the product architecture and built across the frontend and backend. The product connects patient registration, longitudinal records, clinical search, appointments, role-specific workspaces and reporting.

## Product capabilities

- Workspaces for doctors, receptionists, students and administrators.
- Patient profiles, visit histories, appointments and clinical search.
- Clinical decision support within the patient workflow.
- Clinic reporting and population-health insights.

## Engineering considerations

The central challenge is making a broad clinical product usable across roles while keeping the patient record coherent. Clinical decision support must remain understandable to the doctor responsible for the case, and reporting must stay connected to recorded clinical activity.

This public overview intentionally stays at the product level. Clinical algorithms, implementation details and patient data are not published.
