# Personal portfolio — An interactive home for my work

**Role:** Designer & developer  
**Technologies:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, Framer Motion, Netlify  
**Source:** Private

[Public website](https://senagadavid.netlify.app/) · [All projects](../README.md)

## The problem

My work spans software, clinical science, research and writing. The portfolio needed to bring those disciplines into one experience while making individual projects easy to explore.

## My contribution

I built the website as a product in its own right, including project narratives, interactive demonstrations, motion, visual transitions and a private publishing workspace.

## Product capabilities

- Dedicated project pages explaining the problem, approach and technology.
- Interactive demonstrations and 3D elements.
- Research, journal and poetry sections.
- A private workspace for managing published content.
- Responsive navigation and page-specific search metadata.

## Engineering considerations

The challenge is balancing expressive presentation with access to useful information. Component-based pages, shared content models and lazy-loaded routes help organize the experience as it grows. The site also separates public reading from private publishing tools.

This case study showcases the finished experience. The application source and publishing configuration remain private.
