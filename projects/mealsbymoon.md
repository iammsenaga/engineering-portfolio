## The problem

A food business needs a clear path from discovering a dish to placing an order, while also supporting catering enquiries, delivery information and menu administration.

## My contribution

I built across the customer-facing interface and application backend, connecting category-led menu browsing, accounts, carts, ordering and catering journeys.

## Product capabilities

- Menu discovery with category navigation.
- Customer accounts, cart and ordering workflows.
- Catering enquiry paths for different events.
- Administration for menus, orders and related business content.

## Engineering considerations

The interface needs to keep food discovery quick and make the next action clear on a phone. Behind that experience, customer, menu and order state must remain coherent across browsing, account and administrative workflows.

The linked site is a real business website. Its source code, customer records and business configuration are not included here.
